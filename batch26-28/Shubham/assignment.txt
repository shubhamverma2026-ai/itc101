
What is UTF-8?
UTF-8 is a character encoding system used to store and display text on computers. It supports English, numbers, symbols, and many different languages. It is commonly used on websites and in files.
Pros and Cons of CSV and XLSX:
CSV (Comma-Separated Values)

Pros:

Simple and easy to understand.
Takes less storage space.
Easy to share and open in different software.
Works well with large amounts of simple data.
Can be easily used with Python and other programming languages.

Cons:

Does not support formatting like colors, fonts, or borders.
Cannot store multiple sheets in one file.
Does not support formulas, charts, or images.
Data can sometimes get messed up because of commas or encoding issues.

XLSX (Excel File)

Pros:

Supports multiple sheets in one file.
Allows formatting, formulas, charts, and tables.
Better for organizing and presenting data.
Can use Excel features like sorting, filtering, and calculations.
Suitable for detailed data analysis and reports.

Cons:

File size is usually larger than CSV.
Not as simple as CSV for basic data storage.
Some programs may not support all Excel features.
Can be slower when working with very large datasets.
What is Encoding?
Encoding is the process of converting characters or text into a format that a computer can understand and store. Different encoding methods are used to represent different characters. UTF-8 is one of the most commonly used encoding methods.
